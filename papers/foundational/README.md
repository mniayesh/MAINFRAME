### 2. ✅ Hubel & Wiesel (1962) - Visual Cortex Receptive Fields
**File**: `Hubel_Wiesel_1962_Receptive_Fields.pdf` (6.4 MB)

**Citation**: Hubel, D. H., & Wiesel, T. N. (1962). Receptive fields, binocular interaction and functional architecture in the cat's visual cortex. *The Journal of Physiology*, 160(1), 106-154.

**Nobel Prize**: 1981 (Physiology or Medicine)

**Why Critical for MDA**: Proved that circuit computations (Gabor filters for edge detection) are conserved across species. First evidence that computational primitives exist at circuit level.

**Source**: http://www.gatsby.ucl.ac.uk/~lmatthey/teaching/tn1/additional/systems/JPhysiol-1962-Hubel-106-54.pdf

### 3. ✅ Hodgkin & Huxley (1952) - Ion Channel Dynamics
**File**: `Hodgkin_Huxley_1952_Membrane_Current.pdf` (7.8 MB)

**Citation**: Hodgkin, A. L., & Huxley, A. F. (1952). A quantitative description of membrane current and its application to conduction and excitation in nerve. *The Journal of Physiology*, 117(4), 500-544.

**Nobel Prize**: 1963 (Physiology or Medicine)

**Why Critical for MDA**: First discovery of a **universal computational primitive**. Same ion channel equations work for all neurons across all animals. Foundation of all neural computation.

**Source**: https://web.njit.edu/~matveev/Courses/M430_635_F15/HodgkinHuxley_JPhysiol-1952.pdf

### 4. ✅ Georgopoulos et al. (1986) - Population Coding
**File**: `Georgopoulos_1986_Population_Coding.pdf` (1.2 MB)

**Citation**: Georgopoulos, A. P., Schwartz, A. B., & Kettner, R. E. (1986). Neuronal population coding of movement direction. *Science*, 233(4771), 1416-1419.

**Why Critical for MDA**: Entry 196 in catalog. Discovered population vector coding - a reusable primitive for representing continuous variables robustly across neuron populations.

**Formula**: `ŝ = (∑ᵢ rᵢ·dᵢ) / (∑ᵢ rᵢ)`

**Source**: http://brain.phgy.queensu.ca/dorrislab/teaching/PHGY%20826/articles/Herter_Limb/Georgopoulos%20et%20al%20(1986%20-%20Science).pdf

### 5. ✅ Bliss & Lømo (1973) - Long-Term Potentiation
**File**: `Bliss_Lomo_1973_LTP.pdf` (2.5 MB)

**Citation**: Bliss, T. V., & Lømo, T. (1973). Long-lasting potentiation of synaptic transmission in the dentate area of the anaesthetized rabbit following stimulation of the perforant path. *The Journal of Physiology*, 232(2), 331-356.

**Why Critical for MDA**: First experimental validation of Hebbian learning ("cells that fire together, wire together"). Proved learning primitives are universal from sea slugs to humans.

**Source**: https://web.math.princeton.edu/~sswang/literature_general_unsorted/bliss73_J%20Physiol-1973-Bliss-331-56.pdf

### 6. ✅ Sporns et al. (2005) - The Human Connectome
**File**: `Sporns_2005_Human_Connectome.pdf` (147 KB)

**Citation**: Sporns, O., Tononi, G., & Kötter, R. (2005). The human connectome: A structural description of the human brain. *PLoS Computational Biology*, 1(4), e42.

**Why Critical for MDA**: Discovered recurring circuit motifs (feedforward, feedback, lateral inhibition, skip connections) that are reusable connectivity blueprints.

**Source**: https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article/file?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.0010042&type=printable
