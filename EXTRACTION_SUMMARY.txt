TIER 1 ARCHITECTURES (Must-Have for Drosophila Implementation)
================================================================================

These 5 architectures are directly observable in fruit fly neural circuits:

1. STDP Learning (#19)
   Biological: Synaptic plasticity (universal)
   Drosophila: Mushroom body Kenyon cell synapses
   Formula: Δw = A₊ exp(-Δt/τ₊) for causal; -A₋ exp(Δt/τ₋) for anti-causal
   Implementation: Low difficulty

2. Sparse Coding / Oja's Rule (#21)
   Biological: Hebbian learning + weight normalization
   Drosophila: Explains why 2,000 Kenyon cells can classify thousands of odors
   Formula: Δw = η y (x - y w)
   Implementation: Low difficulty

3. Kuramoto Synchronization (#16)
   Biological: Brain gamma synchronization
   Drosophila: Central complex ring neuron phase-locking for heading direction
   Formula: dθᵢ/dt = ωᵢ + (K/N) Σⱼ sin(θⱼ - θᵢ)
   Implementation: Medium difficulty

4. Homeostatic Plasticity (#14)
   Biological: Visual cortex (BCM rule)
   Drosophila: Kenyon cell activity stabilization, antennal lobe adaptation
   Formula: dw = η(post - θ)pre where θ = avg(post²)
   Implementation: Medium difficulty

5. Multi-Objective Loss (#1, #32, #33)
   Biological: E. coli growth optimization
   Drosophila: Flies optimize 40+ behavioral objectives simultaneously
   Formula: loss = Σ wᵢ log(objᵢ/baselineᵢ)
   Implementation: Medium-High difficulty

BIOLOGY-TO-AI MAPPING HIGHLIGHTS
================================================================================

KEY TRANSFERABLE PRINCIPLES:

1. Multi-Objective Optimization
   Biology: Bacteria (65 objectives), Flies (40+ objectives)
   AI: Directly implementable in loss functions
   Advantage: Automatic trade-off learning (no manual weighting)

2. Sparse Distributed Representations
   Biology: Mushroom body (~2,000 Kenyon cells, 10% active)
   AI: PCA + homeostatic plasticity → natural sparsity
   Advantage: Energy efficiency + robustness

3. Local Learning Rules
   Biology: STDP, BCM, Oja (no global backprop)
   AI: Biologically plausible neural networks
   Advantage: Scalable, hardware-implementable

4. Multi-Timescale Processing
   Biology: Fast synaptic (ms) + slow modulation (s-min)
   AI: Dual-channel neurons, multi-timescale RNNs
   Advantage: Solves learning vs recall trade-off

5. Energy Constraints
   Biology: ATP budgeting limits firing
   AI: Automatic sparsity from biophysical constraints
   Advantage: 60-80% sparsity without L1 penalty
